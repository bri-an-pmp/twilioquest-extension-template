**Notary Confi** offers notary services and Spanish ⇄ English translation. Clients book appointments, and missed appointments cost everyone time. Your first job: send a text reminder.

The client's appointment is **Friday, October 16 at 10:00 AM**.

## A good reminder

- Says who it's from (**Notary Confi**).
- Gives the exact day and time.
- Tells the client how to reply: **C** to confirm or **CANCEL** to cancel.
- Fits in one SMS segment (**160 characters** or fewer).

```text
Notary Confi: your appointment is Friday, October 16 at 10:00 AM. Reply C to confirm or CANCEL to cancel.
```

Phone numbers sent to Twilio use **E.164** format: `+` country code and number, no spaces. Use a test number such as `+15125550142`.
